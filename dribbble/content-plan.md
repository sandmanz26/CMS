## POST 1 — Main shot (semua aset, 13 media)

**Title**
SalesHub — Sales CMS for teams that sell in the field

**Description**
SalesHub is a web CMS for sales teams. It's built around one idea: every lead has an owner, a grade and a follow-up SLA, and every deal earns its next stage.

What's inside:
• Leads with clear ownership, A–D quality grades and "last visit / last meeting / last follow-up" at a glance
• Handover with a required reason and full ownership history — no lead gets lost when a rep goes on leave
• Territory map that shows where leads are, who covers each area, and where coverage is thin
• Pipeline checkpoints — deals only move forward when the stage's exit criteria are met (managers can override with a logged reason)
• Revenue targets per rep with pace, projection and gap
• A single, versioned source for sales materials with review flow and must-read tracking

Design notes: a neutral palette with one primary (deep navy) and one secondary (steel blue). Colour is reserved for meaning — success, warning, danger. Navigation lives in a macOS-style dock with a Launchpad for everything else.

Built with React + shadcn/ui.

Thoughts and feedback are welcome 🙌

## POST 2 — Leads with ownership & quality

**Media:** 02-leads.png, 03-lead-detail.png, flow-1-lead-lifecycle.png

**Title**
Leads that always have an owner — SalesHub

**Description**
Every lead in SalesHub answers three questions at a glance: who owns it, how good is it, and when was it last touched.

• Quality grade A–D = engagement score + ICP fit (Hot, Warm, Nurture, Cold)
• Follow-up SLA per grade — A every 3 days, D every 30 days
• "Past SLA" and "Needs attention" views surface leads that are slipping
• Lead detail shows last visit, last meeting, last follow-up and last call side by side, plus the full activity timeline

## POST 3 — Territory map

**Media:** 04-territories.png, 02-leads.png

**Title**
Territory map for a field sales team — SalesHub

**Description**
Where are our leads, and who covers them? The territory map answers both.

• Areas sized by leads, lead value or unassigned leads
• Every lead is a dot, coloured by quality grade; red outline = no owner
• Coverage panel per area: reps, quality mix, leads past SLA, open pipeline
• Flags areas with no active rep (e.g. when the only rep is on leave)

## POST 4 — Pipeline checkpoints

**Media:** 05-pipeline.png, 06-checkpoint-gate.png, flow-2-deal-to-revenue.png

**Title**
Pipeline stages that have to be earned — SalesHub

**Description**
In SalesHub every pipeline stage has exit criteria. A deal can't jump to Qualification until the decision maker is identified, the first meeting is held and the pain point is documented.

• Checkpoint progress on every card ("2/3 checkpoints")
• Dragging a deal forward opens a gate: tick the missing items right there, or…
• Manager override with a written reason, kept on the deal and in the audit log
• Admins edit checkpoints per stage in Settings

Result: a forecast you can actually trust.

## POST 5 — Revenue targets & division health

**Media:** 07-revenue-targets.png, 09-sales-division.png, 01-dashboard.png

**Title**
Revenue targets with pace, not just progress — SalesHub

**Description**
Hitting 60% of target is great on day 10 and bad on day 28. SalesHub measures pace: attainment compared with how much of the month has passed.

• Ahead / On track / At risk / Behind per rep
• Projected = closed revenue + weighted pipeline closing this period
• Gap to target per rep and for the whole team
• Sales Division view: one health score, team comparison, rep scorecard and weekly activity heatmap

**Tags**
analytics, dashboard, kpi, targets, sales, revenue, data visualization, chart, saas, b2b, ui, ux

## POST 6 — Materials & navigation

**Media:** 08-materials.png, 10-launchpad.png

**Title**
One source for sales materials — SalesHub

**Description**
Decks, brochures, price lists and training in one place, with the controls a sales team actually needs.

• Draft → In review → Published, with versions and change notes
• Expiry dates — expired price lists can't be shared
• Must-read items with acknowledgement tracking per rep
• Recommended materials per pipeline stage

Navigation is a macOS-style dock for daily apps, plus a Launchpad for everything else.
